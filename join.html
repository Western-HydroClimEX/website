Join HydroClimEX

We recruit researchers interested in physical processes, statistics and computation, and in seeing their results used in engineering practice. Funded openings are posted here with their start date and funding source; expressions of interest are welcome at any time.

PhD

About four years of thesis research leading to first-author journal papers. Funding details are given with each advertised position.

  • Master's in civil or environmental engineering, hydrology, atmospheric science or a quantitative field
  • Python or R and comfort with large datasets

MESc

A two-year research master's on a defined question inside a funded project.

  • Bachelor's in engineering or a related science
  • Statistics, ideally extremes

Postdoctoral fellows

Postdocs lead research lines. Bring a direction you want to own: coastal processes, emulators, convection-permitting climate or risk.

  • A one to two page research statement
  • CV and three referees

Undergraduate and visiting

Summer research through NSERC USRA, Western USRI and Mitacs Globalink, and visiting students and scholars with their own funding.

  • Western students: ask about USRA in the fall
  • Visitors: send a short proposal
ComputingA national HPC allocation for climate and hydrodynamic modelling

Group meetingsEvery member presents work in progress through the year
ConferencesStudents usually present at national meetings; international travel depends on available support

How to apply

  1. Read two or three of our recent papers in the area you want to work on.
  2. Complete the prospective member form on the faculty page.
  3. Email a CV, unofficial transcripts and a half-page note naming the theme you are interested in and why.
